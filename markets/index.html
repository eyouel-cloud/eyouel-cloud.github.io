Fully Developed Product Areas

Market desk

Plan finance videos

Creators organize stock market education clips by topic, source type, status, and risk disclosure before publishing.

Review workspace

Edit metadata before posting

The app displays the selected video, editable caption, privacy options, interactions, commercial disclosure, AI label, and final consent.

Publishing controls

Direct Post or draft upload

Creators can choose Direct Post through video.publish or draft fallback through video.upload, with status polling after upload.

Compliance

Education-only boundaries

Content is limited to equities, ETFs, market indexes, earnings, macro calendars, and general education, not individualized advice.

How The TikTok Flow Works

Step 1

Creator authorization

The creator signs in with TikTok and grants the exact scopes required for profile identification and Direct Post.

Step 2

Creator info check

The backend queries TikTok creator settings so the app displays available privacy and interaction controls.

Step 3

Direct Post consent

The creator reviews the video, caption, privacy, comments, duet, stitch, disclosure, and AI label before posting.

Content Boundary

Allowed

Financial education

Educational explainers about equities, ETFs, public company earnings, market indexes, economic calendars, and portfolio risk concepts.

Focused

Stock market only

The publishing workflow is limited to equity market education, public company updates, index commentary, and macro calendar context.

Safety

No personalized advice

Videos are general education and commentary. They do not provide individualized investment, tax, legal, or trading advice.
