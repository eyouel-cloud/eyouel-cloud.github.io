The Data Pitch Publisher Posting Workflow

The workflow requires creator review before any original football analysis video is sent to TikTok.

Step 1

Creator logs in

Login Kit connects the creator's TikTok account and confirms the authenticated profile.

Step 2

Creator reviews the video

The app shows the MP4, caption, privacy setting, interactions, disclosures, and ownership checks.

Step 3

Creator posts or drafts

video.publish sends approved Direct Post videos; video.upload sends approved videos to TikTok drafts.
