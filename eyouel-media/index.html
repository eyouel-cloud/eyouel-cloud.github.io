Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Eyouel Media processes TikTok authorization data only to authenticate the creator, retrieve posting settings, prepare creator-owned faith-based motivational videos for review, and publish creator-approved content through TikTok’s Content Posting API.

OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, and client secrets are handled by the server-side posting service and are not embedded in this public website. We do not sell personal information.

Creators control whether to authorize TikTok and whether each post is Direct Posted or uploaded as a draft. Creators may revoke app access from TikTok settings at any time.

Terms Of Service

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Eyouel Media provides a creator-owned workflow for preparing, reviewing, and publishing original faith-based motivational videos. Users are responsible for ensuring they own or have rights to all content they publish.

Content is not posted through Direct Post until the creator reviews the video, caption, privacy setting, interaction settings, disclosures, and confirms final posting consent.

Users may not publish unlawful, misleading, infringing, harmful, or unauthorized content, and may not use the service to post on behalf of another person without authorization.
