Eyouel Media Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Eyouel Media handles information for creators who use the app to review and publish their own original faith-based motivational videos to TikTok.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. App Covered By This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Eyouel Media, available at https://eyouel-cloud.github.io/eyouel-media/. Eyouel Media is a creator-owned web app for preparing, reviewing, and publishing the creator's own original faith-based motivational videos.

2. Information We Process

Eyouel Media may process TikTok authorization information, creator profile information returned through user.info.basic, selected video files, captions, privacy settings, interaction settings, disclosure selections, upload status, and basic app activity needed to operate the posting workflow.

Creator profile information may include the TikTok open id, avatar, and display name if returned by TikTok after authorization.

3. How We Use Information

Eyouel Media uses information to authenticate the creator, display current posting options, prepare content for creator review, initialize Content Posting API requests, upload creator-approved MP4 files, poll publish status, and support draft upload fallback when selected by the creator.

Eyouel Media does not sell personal information and does not use TikTok data to build unrelated advertising profiles.

4. TikTok OAuth And Tokens

When a creator chooses Creator Login, TikTok may return an authorization code to the registered Eyouel Media callback URL. The client secret, access tokens, and refresh tokens are intended to be handled by the secure server-side posting service and are not embedded in this public website.

Creators can revoke Eyouel Media access from their TikTok account settings at any time.

5. Posting Control

Eyouel Media does not Direct Post a video until the authenticated creator reviews the video, caption, privacy setting, interaction settings, disclosures, and confirms final posting consent. The app is intended only for content owned or authorized by the authenticated creator.

6. Sharing And Service Providers

Eyouel Media shares content and posting data with TikTok only as needed to provide the requested Login Kit and Content Posting API functionality. We may use hosting, storage, analytics, and infrastructure providers as needed to operate the service, but only for service operation and security.

7. Retention And Deletion

Eyouel Media keeps app records only as long as needed to provide the service, troubleshoot posting, comply with legal obligations, protect against misuse, and maintain creator-requested posting history. A creator may request deletion of app-held data by contacting support.

8. Security

Eyouel Media uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate for a creator publishing workflow. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect OAuth credentials, posting records, and creator content from unauthorized access.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests related to Eyouel Media, contact the app owner through the support channel used for the Eyouel Media creator account.
