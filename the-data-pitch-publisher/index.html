Fully Developed Product Areas

Content queue

Plan football videos

Creators organize original football analysis videos by topic, status, length, caption, and publishing readiness.

Review workspace

Edit metadata before posting

The app displays the selected video, editable caption, privacy options, interaction controls, and final consent checklist.

Publishing controls

Direct Post or draft upload

Creators can use Direct Post through video.publish or draft fallback through video.upload, with status feedback after upload.

Ownership

Original content only

The workflow is limited to videos owned or cleared by the creator before the creator submits them to TikTok.
