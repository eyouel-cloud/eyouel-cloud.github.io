The Data Pitch Publisher Terms of Service

These terms govern use of The Data Pitch Publisher, a creator-operated web app for preparing, reviewing, and publishing original football analysis videos to the creator's connected TikTok account.

Permitted Use

The Data Pitch Publisher is intended for authorized creators to connect a TikTok account, review original football analysis videos, confirm settings and consent, and transfer approved content through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Content Responsibility

Before any upload, the creator must confirm that the video, caption, visuals, commentary, statistics, and other materials are original, properly licensed, or otherwise cleared for publication.

TikTok Publishing

Direct Post uses video.publish after creator review and consent. Draft upload uses video.upload when the creator chooses to send the reviewed video to TikTok drafts for final publishing in TikTok.

No Unauthorized Account Use

The app should not be used to publish through accounts the creator does not own or manage, and it should not be used to upload infringing, misleading, harmful, unlawful, or unauthorized content.

Availability And Changes

The app may be updated, limited, paused, or discontinued as needed for security, operational, platform, or compliance reasons. TikTok platform availability and API permissions may affect whether a publishing action can be completed.
