The Data Pitch Publisher Privacy Policy

This policy explains how The Data Pitch Publisher handles information used to connect a creator-managed TikTok account and publish original football analysis videos.

Information We Process

The Data Pitch Publisher may process TikTok authorization details, basic TikTok profile information, selected MP4 videos, captions, privacy settings, interaction settings, disclosure settings, and publishing status information needed for the creator-reviewed posting workflow.

How TikTok Access Is Used

The app uses Login Kit to authenticate the connected creator account, user.info.basic to display the authenticated profile before posting, video.publish to submit approved Direct Post videos, and video.upload to send approved videos to TikTok drafts when the creator chooses that fallback.

Storage And Retention

Publishing data is used to operate the workflow, show status, troubleshoot errors, and maintain compliance records. Video content and account information are not sold and are not made public by this website.

Sharing

The Data Pitch Publisher sends video content, captions, and selected posting settings to TikTok only when the creator chooses to publish or create a draft. The app does not provide public access to connected-account data.

Contact

For privacy questions about The Data Pitch Publisher, contact the app owner through the communication channel used to access the creator publishing workflow.
